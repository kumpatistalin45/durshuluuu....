For My Durshu...

Somewhere
along the way...

I never noticed the moment when talking to you became something I started looking forward to.

SOME THINGS CHANGE QUIETLY
01

The conversations

At first, they were just conversations. Words exchanged between two people passing through ordinary days. But somewhere between the jokes, the random thoughts and the endless talks, your words started feeling familiar.

02

The little things

It wasn't always the big moments. Sometimes it was the smallest things — a message, a question, a laugh, a simple "are you okay?" Things that probably meant nothing to you at the time... but somehow stayed with me.

03

The ordinary days

And then the ordinary days started feeling a little different. Because somewhere inside them, there was always a possibility that I would hear from you, talk to you, laugh with you, or simply know that you were there.

04

The realization

I didn't plan for you to become such an important part of my life. I don't think you planned it either. It simply happened — quietly, naturally, beautifully.

AND THEN I REALIZED

I wasn't just living my days anymore...
I was looking for you in them.

"The strange thing about becoming important to someone is that you rarely notice it happening. You only notice when their absence suddenly feels different."

Maybe that's what makes certain friendships so beautiful.

They don't arrive with promises. They don't announce how long they are going to stay.

They simply become a part of you...
before you even realize you were making room for them.

There is more I never said...
