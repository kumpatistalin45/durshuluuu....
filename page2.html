For My Durshu...

And then,
you happened.

“

I don't remember the exact moment you became important to me. Maybe that's what makes it beautiful.

”

You didn't arrive with a warning that you were going to matter this much.

There was no particular day, no dramatic moment, no sign telling me that someday your presence would become something I would quietly look for in my days.

You were simply there.

A conversation. A laugh. A random message. Another ordinary day.

Nothing extraordinary.
At least, that's what I thought then.

Because I didn't know that sometimes the most beautiful things begin as moments we don't think are important enough to remember.

SOMEWHERE ALONG THE WAY

You became a part of my life
without even trying.

Maybe some people aren't meant to enter your life loudly.

Maybe they are meant to arrive quietly...

and stay in places words cannot reach.

There is more I never said...
