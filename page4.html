For My Durshu...

Things
I never say.

Some feelings are not difficult because they are complicated...
They are difficult because they mean too much.

THE WORDS I USUALLY KEEP QUIET

I don't always tell you how much your presence means to me.

Not because it doesn't matter...
but because sometimes saying it out loud makes something beautiful feel too small for the words.

I don't always tell you that some of my better days have your name somewhere inside them.

Sometimes it was just one message. One conversation. One stupid joke.

And somehow, the entire day felt lighter.

I don't always tell you that I notice the little things.

The way you talk. The way you laugh. The things you remember. The things you don't realize I remember.

And I don't think you will ever completely understand

how deeply your existence has touched mine.

Maybe I don't say these things because I am afraid that words will make them ordinary.

And what I feel about having you in my life is never an ordinary thing.

IF I COULD SAY ONLY ONE THING

I hope you know...
you matter more than you realize.

Some people become memories.
Some become chapters.

And then there are those rare people who quietly become a part of the story itself.

There is something I want you to see...
